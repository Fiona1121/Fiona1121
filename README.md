### **Who am I?**

Hey there! I'm Fiona, a frontend engineer and creative technologist who loves crafting smooth, engaging, and downright fun digital experiences. Think of me as the bridge between aesthetics and functionality—because why should you have to choose?

- 🎭 **Versatilist** - Whether it's **frontend engineering, full-stack development, connected devices, or design & prototyping**, I love switching gears and making things happen.
- 🎨 **Design + Code Enthusiast** – I believe great tech should _look_ and _feel_ amazing. Functionality is only half the battle!
- 🛠️ **Tinkerer at heart** – I’m all about exploring new stacks, trying out cool tech, and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible.

**More about me:**

- 🎓 Master's in Computer Science at University of Washington
- ☕️ Coffee Lover – I'm powered by caffeine and creativity.
- 📺 Anime & TV Series Addict – If I love a show, I’ll rewatch it endlessly. (Yes, I still get emotional every time.)
- ⚽️ Loves Volleyball & Soccer – Any excuse to smash or kick things, count me in!
- 🐈‍⬛ I can perfectly fake cat noises—confusing humans and cats alike.
- Let's connect and collaborate! 🚀
